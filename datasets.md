# Dataset Registry & Compliance Documentation

This document is the authoritative registry of public datasets referenced by our platform. Each section documents one dataset and MUST contain a compliant attribution statement as required by our licensing compliance policy (see the open compliance issue).

## 1. SQuAD (Stanford Question Answering Dataset)

- **Registry page:** https://huggingface.co/datasets/rajpurkar/squad
- **Description:** A reading comprehension dataset consisting of questions posed by crowdworkers on a set of Wikipedia articles.

> **Attribution**: This dataset is provided by **rajpurkar** under the **cc-by-sa-4.0** license. Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/rajpurkar/squad

## 2. CNN / DailyMail (Summarization)

- **Registry page:** https://huggingface.co/datasets/abisee/cnn_dailymail
- **Description:** An English-language dataset containing over 300k unique news articles used for summarization research.

> **Attribution**: This dataset is provided by **abisee** under the **apache-2.0** license. Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/abisee/cnn_dailymail

## 3. DailyDialog (Multi-turn Dialogue)

- **Registry page:** https://huggingface.co/datasets/li2017dailydialog/daily_dialog
- **Description:** A high-quality multi-turn dialogue dataset covering various topics about daily life.

> **Attribution**: This dataset is provided by **li2017dailydialog** under the **cc-by-nc-sa-4.0** license. Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/li2017dailydialog/daily_dialog
